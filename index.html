About Us

Shandong Taikai Power Equipment Co., Ltd., founded in 2010, is a national high-tech enterprise integrating design, R&D, production, sales, service, and maintenance. Its main products include on-load tap changers (OLTC)(35-220kV), fiberglass reinforced plastic dry-type bushings (FRP) (12-330kV), and neutral point complete set devices(110-220kV).

Through cooperation with major domestic transformer companies, its terminal products have earned the trust of major players in the power industry, are utilized by companies such as State Grid, Southern Grid, and involved in various industries such as coal, petroleum, steel, smelting, chemical engineering, railway and photovoltaic/wind power.

The products are exported to more than 20 countries including Thailand, Mongolia, Uzbekistan, Nicaragua, Oman and Zimbabwe,etc.
